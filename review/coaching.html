Coaching School Leaders

Professional development for school leaders often involves gathering school leaders for workshops and group training to learn new ideas and skills. While this is valuable, school leaders also benefit from separate, individual conversations to help them think through challenges and apply what they are learning to their school’s unique context. We call this coaching. Coaching provides a supportive and confidential space where leaders, even experienced ones, can process concerns and make decisions.

In the Global South, where the challenges of school leadership are often compounded by severe resource constraints, school leader coaching is not common, but it can be transformative.

For organisations designing coaching

A Practice Guide

This guide is designed as a flexible resource to support the development of school leader coaching in the Global South. It is aimed at organisations looking to design or refine their school leader support, and may also be useful to others interested in coaching school leaders.

Questions the guide works through
  • Who provides the coaching?
  • Who chooses the focus of the coaching?
  • What happens in the coaching?
  • Is the coaching in a group or individual?
  • What is an ideal ratio of coaches to school leaders?
  • Do school leaders have a choice?
