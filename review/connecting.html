Connecting School Leaders

GSL believes that any school leadership program design should balance theory and practice and be relevant to each school's context. Further, we believe training is most effective if it utilizes the 3C’s -

  • Strong Content grounded in evidence and relevant to each school's context
  • Coaching for school leaders by those who know the context
  • Community between school leaders in a particular region or country

We believe that School Leaders will find incentives and inspiration to enhance their practice if they have access to relevant knowledge and can share their experiences, problems, and achievements with a community of peers. Our initial user research indicates that School Leaders draw motivation, direction, and encouragement from peer communities.

The Online Community Guide provides guidance across three key stages - Before, During, and After the Launch of an online community. In the report Building Online Communities for School Leaders, we share our findings and lessons from the pilot testing and a high-level overview of some best practices.

Building Online Communities for School Leaders

What we learned from running WhatsApp communities for School Leaders. To explore how we might foster a sense of community in an online setting, we piloted three WhatsApp-based communities in 2021-22.

The three pilot communities
  • Global community: 167 School Leaders from 35 countries, eight months
  • National community: 156 School Leaders from 25 states in India, six weeks
  • Regional community: 80 Educational Officers, including School Leaders, from Nagapattinam district, Tamil Nadu, eight weeks
